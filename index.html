01 — How it works

Your order is a ripple nobody can read.

01

Post it hidden

Pick a pump.fun coin, buy or sell, and a size. Your wallet signs a message, not a transaction. The order never shows up in any list, feed or book.

02

Matched at mid

When another wallet's order crosses yours, D4RK pairs them at the coin's mid price, inside both your limits. Nobody else learns it happened.

03

One transaction

Coin goes one way, SOL the other, inside a single transaction. Both wallets sign it. If either side can't pay, nothing moves.

04

No chart, no candle

The swap never touches the pool. No price impact, no slippage, and no bot sees it coming. The chart never knows.

03 — Trade in the dark

05 — The tech

One transaction. Two signatures. No program.

1

Advance a nonce

The first signer's durable nonce keeps the trade valid while the other side gets round to signing.

2

Coin leg

The seller's coins move straight into the buyer's own token account.

3

SOL leg

The buyer's SOL moves straight to the seller's wallet.

4

Both sign

It's one transaction, so it settles whole or not at all. Nobody can take one leg and walk.

No custody

D4RK never holds a coin or a lamport. Your order is a signed message; your tokens stay in your wallet until the trade settles.

No program

Every trade uses only Solana's own System, Token and Memo programs. Nothing new to audit, nothing to drain.

Simulated first

Every trade runs on mainnet in simulation before either wallet signs. If a leg can't pay, you see why.

Mid from the market

The price is the coin's live mid, read at the moment of the match. Each side's limit guards it.

06 — What D4RK can't do

The dark has rules too.

  • It needs a counterparty. An order only fills when another wallet wants the other side. Early on, most orders will wait, then expire.
  • The second signature takes time. The price can move between the match and the second signature. Either side can walk away before signing.
  • Settled trades are public. Before a trade, nobody can see it. After it settles, it's a Solana transaction like any other.
  • Some coins can't trade. Tokens with transfer fees, transfer hooks or frozen accounts are refused before you sign.
